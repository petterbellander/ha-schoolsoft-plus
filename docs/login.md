# Getting a SchoolSoft username and password

SchoolSoft Plus logs in with a guardian's **username and password**. Many guardians only ever log in to SchoolSoft with BankID and have never had a password, but you can request one yourself from your school's login page. It takes a few minutes and two e-mails.

You need access to the e-mail address registered for you as a guardian in SchoolSoft. If you're not sure which address that is, log in with BankID and check or update it in your SchoolSoft profile first.

## 1. Get your username

1. Open your school's SchoolSoft login page, `https://sms.schoolsoft.se/<school>/` (the same `<school>` you enter when setting up the integration).
2. Click **Behöver du hjälp med inloggningen?** ("Need help logging in?").
3. Click **Glömt användarnamnet?** ("Forgot your username?").
4. Choose **Vårdnadshavare** (guardian) under *Jag är*, enter your e-mail address and click **Skicka användarnamn**.

Your username is sent to that e-mail address. Don't be surprised if it looks like your child's school e-mail address; that is how some schools set up guardian accounts.

## 2. Get a password

1. Go back to the login page and click **Behöver du hjälp med inloggningen?** again.
2. Choose **Vårdnadshavare**, enter the username from step 1 and your e-mail address, and click **Skicka nytt lösenord**.

A password is sent to your e-mail. Use it together with the username when you set up SchoolSoft Plus.

## Troubleshooting

- **No e-mail arrives:** check your spam folder. If nothing turns up, a different e-mail address (or none) is probably registered for you. Log in to SchoolSoft with BankID, add or correct your e-mail address in your profile, then try again.
- **Login fails in Home Assistant but works on the website:** make sure you picked the right school name and that you log in as a guardian, not with a student account.
